Overview

In a multidisciplinary team with engineering and business students, we explored “how might we help businesses participate in the online game economy”. After months of brainstorming, critiques, data collection, risk assessment and experimental prototyping, the idea became an in-game item designer-matching platform.

Mission. Atomo connects gamers and content creators with a talented and diverse group of digital artists and designers, so that the average person can creatively express themselves online.

03Experiments

Why we pivoted

Experimental prototypingCustomer interviews

We tested the idea with real gamers and designers before building it — and what we learned changed the product.

7Experimental designs
80Experiment participants
72Customer interviews

Key learnings

  • Aligning customer design requests to stable AI art is impractical
  • Customers need guidance to discover their design preferences
  • Gamers’ willingness to pay is high when prices are low

Product iterations

  • Focus design services on original character creation
  • Create products around microtransactions
  • Implement a visual process of preference discovery
