Overview

Artisk instantly produces logos, product imagery and complete visual identities for any brand. I redesigned its homepage and generated the marketing visuals the new site needed.

01The brief

Too dark, too heavy

The existing site was black-dominant with a green accent. The founder wanted a direction that felt lighter, airier and less demanding — closer to a modern AI product.

02Research & direction

Three directions, one choice

Team researchVisual direction

With the other designers I studied how modern web products handle layout, hierarchy and lightness, and we distilled three directions.

01

Explore

02

03

Choose

Card-based modern style with the brand’s green accent.

04

Why

It mirrors the interface of the generator itself — site and product feel like one system.

03My role

Homepage: from wireframe to final

WireframingLayout

The redesign covered Home, About, Inspiration, Pricing and Blog. I owned the homepage: wireframe iterations to lock the layout, finalised with a senior designer, then refined and filled with visuals.
