Problem

Traditional design tools and existing AI tools have steep learning curves. How might we create an affordable, user-friendly AI design tool that helps nonprofits and small businesses with limited resources design faster and better?

Non-designersSmall businesses & startupsNonprofitsEducational institutionsLess tech-savvy users
01Solution

Two core functions

Each one answers a pain point non-designers described in our research.

Function 01

Information collection

Materials, colour, style and keywords are gathered through questionnaire-style steps — so nobody has to write a long prompt full of art jargon.

Function 02

Result modification

A selection tool plus keywords for small tweaks, right on the result — no layers, and no jumping between different software.

02Research

Interviews and competitors

6 user interviewsCompetitive analysis

Six interviews with people from non-design backgrounds to basic design experience, and an analysis of four AI-powered tools.

Interviews

Ideation is hard

Many struggled to come up with fresh ideas.

Interviews

Little onboarding

Features were hard to find; tools like Canva were hard to adapt for complex tasks.

Interviews

Need for flexibility

People wanted diverse functions in one place.

Midjourney

Fast ideation and unique visuals, but little control or precision.

Canva

Accessible and collaborative; limited by template quality and unclear onboarding.

Uizard

Rapid prototyping and real-time collaboration; learning curve and pricing concerns.

GitHub Copilot

Useful AI-assisted workflow ideas; risks over-reliance and privacy issues.

Takeaway: balance creative idea generation with customisation, add clear in-app tutorials, and support an iterative, user-driven process.

04Paper prototype

Tested on paper first

4 participantsPaper prototype test

Four participants tried the information-collection and modification flows on paper.

What we found

  • Unclear input fields and a confusing upload feature
  • No guidance for design terminology
  • Information overload and unclear icons on the result page
  • Copyright concerns; no undo

What we changed

  • Example prompts and tooltips
  • Tutorials
  • Version history
  • Multiple logo results with similarity checks
