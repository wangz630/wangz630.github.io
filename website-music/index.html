Overview

This is another experimental website that translates sound into geometric shapes. Each keyboard key corresponds to a specific sound, generating a unique shape. The music you create becomes a personalized painting. Even if you can't hear the music, the abstract painting on the screen provides a visual representation of the music in a different form.
